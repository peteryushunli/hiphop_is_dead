    Methods & sources

    A reproducible first edition.

    The corpus

    English lyrics, released from 1990 through September 30, 2026. The cohort combines The Pudding’s published artist roster with newer artists and comparison genres. It is not a random sample or a chart census. Genre labels apply to artists’ catalogs and crossover artists can affect the comparison.

    Historical lyrics come from theelderemo/genius-lyrics-cleaned, derived from the Genius Song Lyrics dataset. The recent supplement checks up to 100 popularity-sorted catalog entries for each selected contemporary artist and retains up to 40 eligible songs from 2023 onward.

    The calculations

    Words are lowercased and section labels removed. Apostrophes and slang are retained. Exact lyric duplicates and duplicate artist/title pairs are removed; likely remixes, live versions, demos, and non-song entries are excluded. Songs need at least 50 words.

    Frequency ratios have a small smoothing adjustment and minimum song, artist, and token support. The year-matched option weights comparison-year frequencies to the hip-hop token distribution. The repeated-line option removes identical lines within each song.

    The artist map

    Each artist needs 20 songs. Signature words use sublinear TF-IDF, keeping words used by at least 10% of eligible hip-hop artists. Each artist’s own name and its components are omitted from their signature list. Cosine similarity determines neighbors. SVD followed by t-SNE produces the map, using a fixed seed.

    Features, hooks, and samples belong to the credited catalog; individual verses are not assigned to performers. Similarity measures vocabulary, not talent, influence, subject matter, or musical sound.

    What this can—and cannot—say

    These are exploratory rankings for the collected sample. Dates and genre labels still need spot checks. Artist output, missing songs, transcription practices, and the change of source after 2022 can affect the results. A missing word is not proof an artist never used it.

    The source lyrics remain local. This explorer contains aggregate statistics only. Download the coverage table, word rankings, or collection audit.
